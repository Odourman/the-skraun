## New source exact P1 color/bitmap window control
Actual R3 source (8 bytes each): K1 `18 3C 7E DB 5A 24 66 42`; K2 `24 7E 5A DB 7E 3C 66 81`; Ranger `18 3C 7E 5A 7E 18 24 42`. For accepted N6 comparison, K1/K2 have the SAME symbolic blue TIA byte; Ranger has a DIFFERENT symbolic world-camo byte (actual color value not finalized). In S2 close, K2 X83 row3 `DB` ends lit at X90 and Ranger X101 row3 `5A` first lights at X102, leaving **11 blank pixel clocks**. A *naive rule* requiring TWO complete 3-cycle store instructions wholly within this gap FAILS (18 clocks). **Important correction:** that is NOT a physical theorem: a preloaded first STA can *start while outgoing pixels still draw* and finish after the last lit pixel; two 3-cycle store-completions can, in principle, fit (e.g. absolute TIA clocks159 and168). Our optimistic write-completion model finds **zero** bare two-write fails among the 16 adjacent-source-row checks in S2 close and S3. Actual independent X-start, register data loads, NUSIZ timing and PF remain unproven. The companion replay prints both strict and optimistic results to avoid a false universal negative.
