## C. Bindande AUDIO DIRECTION: occult woodland magic plus punchy arcade cues

Use two independent original TIA audio channels: AUDC0/1 (sound type 0–15), AUDF0/1 (frequency divider 0–31), AUDV0/1 (volume 0–15). Implement a concise priority-aware, table- or envelope-driven two-voice engine in VBLANK/overscan, not ad hoc one-note static buzzes and not costly visible-kernel sound writes. Each independent voice needs a current event ID, priority, frame/step and remaining duration, with small state memory. Prefer packed effect byte tables and real volume/pitch envelopes.

**Specific sound events and desired character (to be auditioned, not untested fixed pitches):**
- Halo start: eerie fluttering/sweeping, short pulse and fading shimmer; L3 feels deeper/stronger, repeated FIRE not unbearable.
- Knight 24f WARNING: two short metallic, sharp audible signals that convey imminent danger. It must remain audible over ongoing halo. Knight SWING: sharp whoosh/crack timed to actual seven-frame active sword, distinct from warning.
- Ranger DRAW: faint pulled bow tension; real RELEASE: plucked string twang and quick air noise. Arrow blocked/destroyed: small dry tick; projectile hit player: clearly dangerous rasp.
- Orb: rising three-tone magical chime, with stronger flourish on L3 acquisition. Enemy kill: brief rough descending chirp. Player level loss: coarse descending low growl; final life loss: heavier slower variant; extra life: bright four-tone fanfare.
- Stage clear: 3–4 note mysterious fragment plus subtle tail during transition, not looping during combat. New stage optional soft 1-note onset; Game Over: low falling dissonant phrase then silence. FIRE restart: clear opening cue.
- Sound palette: mysterious dark, slightly surreal, magic/forest/medieval, but most importantly game actions audibly distinct and readable.
